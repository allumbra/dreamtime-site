Miner Sweeper

Miner Sweeper is Minesweeper rebuilt as a first-person 3D logic puzzle. You walk around a floating blocky structure, a cottage, a castle, a tractor or a famous landmark, and dig it apart block by block. Every block you uncover becomes a number cube that counts the mines among the 26 blocks touching it, by face, edge or corner. Read the numbers, flag the mines and clear every safe block. Every layout is generated so that logic alone solves it after your first dig: no guessing. Made by Dreamtime, a one-person independent studio.

Key features

  • Minesweeper in first-person 3DWalk around the structure, dig blocks and read number cubes that count the mines touching each block in all three dimensions. Flag every mine to win.
  • Pure logic, no guessingEvery puzzle is generated so it can be solved by reasoning alone after the first dig. Blow up? Retry the same layout, mines in the same places.
  • Over 90 hand-built voxel structuresCottages, cars, castles, landmarks, animals, rockets and kitchen appliances, grouped into collections: a free Starter and Classics collection plus optional packs.
  • Daily puzzles at four difficultiesA new puzzle every day at Beginner, Easy, Normal and Hard, the same for everyone, with a streak and online leaderboards.
  • The Infinite WellDig down a shaft that never ends and see how deep you can get.
  • A world that changes with the monthsSeasonal looks around the puzzle (blossom in spring, snow in winter) and calm, original ambient music.
  • Easy to learnAn interactive How to Play guide shows how the 3D numbers work, step by step. Plays offline.
