Miner Sweeper brings no-guess minesweeper to 3D, out now on Android

Miner Sweeper is out now on Google Play. It's minesweeper in three dimensions, and every puzzle can be solved without guessing.

You walk around blocky cars, castles and rockets with a rock hammer and dig them apart one block at a time. Each block you dig shows a number: how many mines lie among the 26 blocks touching it, by face, edge or corner. Read the numbers, flag the mines, clear the rest.

There are 108 puzzles in themed packs: vehicles, famous buildings, animals, space missions, kitchen gadgets, retro game tributes and more. Every new game gives you a fresh layout of mines.

There's a new puzzle every day at four difficulty levels. Everyone plays the same puzzle at each difficulty, and a leaderboard shows how your time compares. Starting or retrying a daily costs one token. You start with 10 tokens and get 2 free each day.

Stuck? Tap Hint and the game walks you through the next logical move (dailies are hint-free). Blew up? Try the same layout again, with the mines in the same places. For a change of pace, dig down the Infinite Well and see how deep you can go. Luck plays a part there too.

Miner Sweeper is free to download. The starter pack and the classics are free. Buy any other pack and it's yours to keep. Ads are optional and never interrupt a puzzle. It plays offline, in 28 languages.
