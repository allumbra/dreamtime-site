Miner Sweeper

What's new

Miner Sweeper is in testing on Android and iPhone/iPad. Here's what changed for testers, newest first.

November 5, 2026

Score bar icons and your daily rank

  • The bar at the top of the game now shows icons only: a mine for mines left, a block for blocks left to dig, and a clock.
  • Win a daily puzzle and the card tells you where you placed, like “You're #3 today on Normal.”
  • You also see today's top 3, and a Leaderboard button opens the whole board.
  • Offline? Your rank shows up by itself once you're back online.
November 4, 2026

The Retro Board, and hints that stay put

  • New in Classics: the Retro Board, a big flat board in the old-school look, lying on the ground.
  • You start above it, looking down. The joystick moves you across the board, up and down zoom in and out, and you can dig and flag from any height.
  • The hammer sits a little further right, out of your way.
  • A hint now stays on its blocks while you move and look around, and you can keep playing with it open.
  • If the hint is out of view, the camera flies you there. Use ‹ › to switch between hints.
November 3, 2026

Hints, Tips and yesterday's solutions

  • Stuck? Tap the new ? button for a free hint, step by step: where to look, why it works, then it can do the move for you. Hints are in collections and the Infinite Well, not in dailies.
  • A hint also spots a flag on a safe block and helps you fix it.
  • Tips teach the common moves: What if? (pretend a block is a mine and see if a number breaks), the 1-1, the 1-2, the 1-2-1 and more shortcuts.
  • The Daily screen has Yesterday's solutions: step through yesterday's puzzles with every move explained.
  • Also new: a Hint cube fade setting that softens far-away hint cubes on busy boards, softer button and flag sounds, and the mine block as the mine icon.
November 1, 2026

Leaderboards, volume sliders and tokens

  • Leaderboards now have a page for each daily difficulty, with Today and Yesterday.
  • Settings → Sound has two sliders: one for music, one for sound effects. Slide one to zero to turn it off.
  • Free daily tokens are now 2 a day, plus 1 more when you clear your first daily.
October 29, 2026

Feedback, reminders and a new splash screen

  • Found a bug or have an idea? Send feedback from Settings or the pause menu: pick Bug, Idea, Question or Other and write a few words.
  • Daily-puzzle reminders, if you'd like them: one gentle reminder on days you haven't played yet, at a time you choose.
  • A new splash screen when the game starts, a dimmed take on the in-game look.
October 28, 2026

Original music

  • Miner Sweeper has its own calm background music: two ambient pieces, Clover and Clay and Starlit Quarry, that take turns with slow fades and quiet gaps.
  • Both were made for the game: soft piano, warm pads and a little wind, water, birds and crickets.
  • Prefer quiet? Turn the music down or off in Settings → Sound.

Something not right, or an idea? Use Feedback in the game, or write to support@dreamti.me.
