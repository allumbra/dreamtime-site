Miner Sweeper

Minesweeper rebuilt as a first-person 3D logic puzzle. Dig into blocky castles, landmarks, animals and rockets, one block at a time.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play

How to play ↓

Trailer

25 seconds of digging, flagging and clearing, with the game's own sounds.

How to play

01

Dig a block

Every block you uncover shows a number: how many hidden mines lie among the 26 blocks touching it, by face, edge or corner.

02

Flag the mines

Work out where the mines must be and flag them. Every layout is made so logic alone solves it after your first dig. No guessing needed.

03

Clear the structure

Clear every safe block to win. Blow up? Try the same layout again, with the mines in the same places.

Gameplay

Over 90 hand-built voxel structures, from cottages and cars to famous landmarks, grouped into collections. Each new game deals a fresh no-guess layout.

A puzzle every day
A new daily puzzle at four difficulties, the same for everyone, with a streak and a leaderboard.
Four difficulties
Beginner, Easy, Normal and Hard. Beginner and Easy show clues on the outside of the structure.
Infinite Well
Dig down a shaft that never ends and see how deep you can go.
Fair and friendly
Free to start, works offline. Rewarded ads are optional and never interrupt a puzzle.
