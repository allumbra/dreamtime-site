Privacy Policy — Tridice

Effective November 14, 2026 · Android app com.dreamtime.tridice

Tridice is a game by Dreamtime LLC ("Dreamtime," "we," or "us"). This policy describes the information the Tridice app for Android uses, its online features, Google Play purchases and Google's advertising services. Before an iOS version is released, we will update this policy to cover it.

In short: you don't need an account or a name to play. We collect only what the game's features need. Your name and scores appear on the public leaderboards only if you join them, and your country only if you choose to show it. Every ad is non-personalized, the app does not use your device's advertising ID, and we do not sell or share your personal information. You can ask us to see or delete your data at support@dreamti.me.

Information on your device

Your settings, token balance, saved games, local high scores and display name (if you choose one) are stored on your device. Removing the app may remove these files, subject to your device's backup settings. It does not delete records held online (see Deletion).

Your game account

So the daily challenge, your tokens and your purchases work, the game signs in to a game account on Microsoft PlayFab without asking you for any personal details. It signs in with your device's Android ID. On current Android versions this value is specific to apps from Dreamtime on your device, and it stays the same if you reinstall Tridice, so your purchases and tokens come back with your account. It is not your advertising ID, and we don't use it for advertising.

PlayFab stores, for this account: the identifier above, your token balance and the tokens you have bought, the products you own and, only if you join the leaderboards, your display name and scores. When the app signs in, PlayFab receives your IP address and records an approximate location (such as your country) derived from it, as part of operating its service. We use that location only if you turn on Show my country (below).

Leaderboards (optional)

The leaderboards are public. After a game, Tridice asks once whether you want to join them, with a generated name you can change. If you choose Not now, your scores are not sent and your name appears on no leaderboard. You can join or leave at any time in Settings → Gameplay → Show me on leaderboards.

While you are on the leaderboards, your display name, scores and rank can be seen by anyone who plays Tridice. Please don't use your real name or anything that identifies you. When you leave, no new scores are sent and your name on the leaderboards is replaced with a generated one. Scores you already posted stay, under that generated name, until the daily and weekly boards reset; to have your all-time entry removed, email us (see Deletion).

Showing your country (optional)

Show my country in Settings is off unless you turn it on. When it is on, your country (worked out from your IP address when you sign in) is shown next to your name and you are included in the Country leaderboard, where you see players from your country who also chose to show theirs. When you turn it off, we remove the saved country and you leave the Country leaderboard. Other players' countries are never shown unless they chose to show them.

Purchases

Google Play processes purchases. The game receives the product and a purchase token from Google Play and sends it to PlayFab, which checks it with Google and records what you own, so honest purchases are delivered and can be restored. We never receive your payment-card or bank details. Google's purchase records are covered by Google's policies, and deleting game data does not issue a refund.

Rewarded ads

Tridice uses Google AdMob for rewarded ads that you choose to watch for tokens. Every ad is requested with Google's most private settings, for every player in every region: non-personalized, treated as directed to children, treated as from a user under the age of consent, and limited to content rated for general audiences. Non-personalized ads are chosen from context, such as the app and your general location, not from a profile of your interests. The Android app does not request the advertising ID permission, so Google's advertising SDK cannot read your advertising ID through it. Because no ad is personalized, the game does not show an ad consent form.

Even for these ads, Google's advertising SDK can collect your IP address (which indicates a general location), information about your device and the app, interactions with ads, diagnostics, and identifiers that are not the advertising ID, such as Android's app set ID. Google uses this to deliver and measure ads, limit how often an ad is shown and prevent fraud. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Feedback

Settings → Help → Send feedback opens an email to support@dreamti.me. The subject line carries only the game version and your platform; nothing else is added. We use what you write, and your email address, to answer you and to improve the game.

Service providers and sharing

Microsoft PlayFab hosts the game accounts, purchase checks and leaderboards. Google provides Google Play billing and advertising. They process information to run these features and may do so outside your country. Their own policies describe their processing:

  • Microsoft Privacy Statement
  • Google Privacy Policy

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for targeted (cross-context behavioral) advertising. Tridice does not ask for access to your contacts, camera, microphone or precise location.

Keeping and deleting your data

Game account records stay online so your tokens and purchases can be restored, until you ask us to delete them. To delete your Tridice game account and its data, including leaderboard entries, email support@dreamti.me from the device's mail app or tell us your display name and roughly when you played, so we can find the account. You don't need to reinstall the game to ask. Deleting the account does not delete Google's purchase records or issue a refund.

Children

Tridice is made for a general audience. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. To protect younger players anyway, every ad is requested as child-directed and non-personalized, the leaderboards and country are off until a player turns them on, and the game suggests a generated name instead of a real one. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has given us personal information, such as a real name on a leaderboard, email support@dreamti.me and we will delete it.

Your privacy rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us what information we hold about your Tridice game account, for a copy of it, to correct it or to delete it. Email support@dreamti.me. We may need to confirm that the account is yours, and we will not treat you differently for asking.

European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland. Under the GDPR and the UK GDPR you can also restrict or object to our processing and receive your data in a portable format. We use your information to provide the game you asked for (the game account, tokens, daily challenge and purchases), for our legitimate interests in keeping the game secure, preventing fraud and funding it with non-personalized ads, and with your consent for the leaderboards and showing your country, which you can withdraw at any time in Settings. You can complain to your local data protection authority. We answer requests within one month.

California and other US states. Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (as amended by the CPRA) and similar state laws, you can ask to know what personal information we collect, use and disclose, and to have it corrected or deleted. We do not sell or share personal information for targeted advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of. An authorized agent can make a request for you. We answer requests within 45 days.

Security

The game's online requests use HTTPS, and purchases are checked on the server before anything is unlocked. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure.

Changes to this policy

When the game starts collecting or using information in a new way, we update this policy first and change its effective date. Features that need personal data stay optional.
