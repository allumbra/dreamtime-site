Privacy Policy — Lexicriss

Effective November 14, 2026 · Android and iOS apps

Lexicriss is a word game by Dreamtime LLC ("Dreamtime," "we," or "us"). It was previously called RogueWords. This policy describes the information the Lexicriss apps for Android and iOS use, their online features and Google's advertising services.

In short: nothing is sent to our game server until you choose Play online, and you can turn online play off at any time. You never need a name, email address or password. Every ad is non-personalized, the game does not use your advertising ID, and we do not sell or share your personal information. You can ask us to see or delete your data at support@dreamti.me.

Information on your device

Your saved games, settings, statistics, the words you have found and your choice about online play are stored on your device. Removing the app may remove these files, subject to your device's backup settings. It does not delete records held online (see Deletion).

Online play (your choice)

The first time you open Lexicriss, it asks Play online? before anything is sent. Online play gets each day's puzzle, the game's word list and your tokens from our game server. If you choose Not now, the game sends nothing to our game server; you can still finish a puzzle already saved on your device, but you can't start a new daily puzzle. You can change your choice at any time under Privacy on the main menu. Turning online play off ends the game's session, and nothing more is sent until you turn it back on.

Your game account

When online play is on, the game signs in to a guest game account on Microsoft PlayFab without asking you for any personal details. It signs in with an identifier from your device: on Android, the Android ID, which on current Android versions is specific to apps from Dreamtime on your device; on iOS, Apple's identifier for vendor, which is also specific to apps from Dreamtime. Neither is your advertising ID, and we don't use either for advertising.

PlayFab stores, for this account: the identifier above, your token balance and how you spend tokens, your daily bonus claims and their times, rewards granted (such as tokens for an ad you chose to watch) and any early-access entitlements. We use this to keep your tokens in sync, deliver bonuses and rewards, and prevent duplicate grants or abuse. When the app signs in, PlayFab receives your IP address and may record an approximate location (such as your country) derived from it, as part of operating its service. We don't use that location.

The game downloads its word list from our servers on Microsoft Azure. To do this, it asks PlayFab for a download link and then downloads the file. These requests include your game account identifier and are logged by our service providers.

Rewarded ads

Lexicriss can offer rewarded ads from Google AdMob that you choose to watch for tokens. Ads only start when online play is on, since our server grants the reward. Every ad is requested with Google's most private settings, for every player in every region: non-personalized, treated as directed to children, treated as from a user under the age of consent, and limited to content rated for general audiences. Non-personalized ads are chosen from context, such as the app and your general location, not from a profile of your interests. The game does not ask iOS for permission to track you and does not use your advertising ID. Before any ad loads, the game checks with Google's consent tool and shows Google's consent message if Google says it is required.

Even for these ads, Google's advertising SDK can collect your IP address (which indicates a general location), information about your device and the app, interactions with ads, diagnostics, and identifiers that are not the advertising ID, such as Android's app set ID or Apple's identifier for vendor. Google uses this to deliver and measure ads, limit how often an ad is shown and prevent fraud. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

Lexicriss does not sell anything in the app today. Before we add in-app purchases, we will update this policy.

Feedback

Feedback on the main menu opens an email to support@dreamti.me. The subject line carries only the game version and your platform; nothing else is added. We use what you write, and your email address, to answer you and to improve the game.

Service providers and sharing

Microsoft PlayFab hosts the game accounts and Microsoft Azure hosts the word list. Google provides advertising. If you download Lexicriss from Google Play or the App Store, Google or Apple also process information under their own policies. These providers process information to run these features and may do so outside your country. Their own policies describe their processing:

  • Microsoft Privacy Statement
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple Privacy Policy

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for targeted (cross-context behavioral) advertising. Lexicriss does not ask for access to your contacts, camera, microphone or precise location, and it has no public leaderboards.

Keeping and deleting your data

Game account records stay online so your tokens can be kept in sync, until you ask us to delete them. To delete your Lexicriss game account and its data, email support@dreamti.me. Tell us your device model and roughly when you played, so we can find the account. Don't send passwords, codes or payment details. We may ask for information to confirm the account is yours, and we will tell you about any record we must keep and why. Turning online play off stops new data being sent but does not delete what is already held, so email us for that too.

Children

Lexicriss is made for a general audience. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. To protect younger players anyway, nothing is sent until online play is chosen, the game never asks for a name or contact details, and every ad is requested as child-directed and non-personalized. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has given us personal information, email support@dreamti.me and we will delete it.

Your privacy rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us what information we hold about your Lexicriss game account, for a copy of it, to correct it or to delete it. Email support@dreamti.me. We may need to confirm that the account is yours, and we will not treat you differently for asking.

European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland. Under the GDPR and the UK GDPR you can also restrict or object to our processing and receive your data in a portable format. We use your information with your consent for online play (which you can withdraw at any time under Privacy on the main menu), to provide the online game you asked for (the game account, tokens and daily puzzle), and for our legitimate interests in keeping the game secure, preventing fraud and funding it with non-personalized ads. You can complain to your local data protection authority. We answer requests within one month.

California and other US states. Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (as amended by the CPRA) and similar state laws, you can ask to know what personal information we collect, use and disclose, and to have it corrected or deleted. We do not sell or share personal information for targeted advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of. An authorized agent can make a request for you. We answer requests within 45 days.

Security

The game's online requests use HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure.

Changes to this policy

When the game starts collecting or using information in a new way, we update this policy first and change its effective date. Features that need personal data stay optional.
