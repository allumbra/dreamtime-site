How to play ↓

How to play

01

Follow the numbers

Tap a chain of neighbouring cells. When their numbers add up to the target, you've made a plot.

02

Leave a little gold

The last cell of each plot turns gold. Plan where you finish so your gold cells touch and form islands.

03

Find another way

Each island multiplies its numbers together. Try another solution and add to your score for the puzzle.

Gameplay

A stranded cell can send you back to find a different path. Where you finish matters as much as how you get there.
