Support — Patida

Need help with Patida, found a bug, or have a suggestion? Email support@dreamti.me. Please include your device model and, if you can, a screenshot.

Purchases

Puzzle packs and the Everything Bundle can be restored on a new device with Shop → Restore purchases. Sign in with Game Center (iPhone and iPad) or Google Play Games (Android) in Settings to keep your tokens and packs on all your devices. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their store policies.

Progress and sign-in

Patida works without an account. Signing in from Settings lets you recover your progress if you reinstall or switch devices.

Privacy and account deletion

See the privacy policy. To delete your game account and data, use Settings → Privacy & account deletion in the game or follow these steps.
