What's new

Patida is in testing on Android. Here's what changed in each test build, newest first.

Changes that came from your feedback are marked You asked.

October 9, 2026 · version 0.1.17

Privacy and account deletion in Settings

  • Settings → Account has a Privacy & account deletion link. It opens how to delete your Patida account and data.
October 9, 2026 · version 0.1.16

Ad tokens right away

  • Watch a rewarded ad to the end and your 15 tokens are ready at once, with a “15 tokens added” message. They're kept if you close the game.
  • Closing an ad early doesn't add tokens.
October 8, 2026 · version 0.1.15

More room in the Shop and Settings

  • The Shop says “Loading ad… Please wait” while an ad loads, and no longer shows old messages from an earlier try.
  • The Shop and Settings have more space around their edges, and nothing hides behind the Back button any more.
October 8, 2026 · version 0.1.14

Steadier rewarded ads

  • The Shop no longer flashes “unavailable” while an ad is getting ready, and it keeps the right state if you reopen it.
  • Daily ad progress follows the GMT day. Five ads in a day still unlock unlimited undos for 12 hours.
October 7, 2026 · version 0.1.13

Smoother sign-in

  • Signing in with Google and picking your player name work better.
  • The game fits around notches and rounded screen corners.
September 22, 2026 · version 0.1.11

Leaderboard and Next Puzzle on the solved screen

  • Solved a puzzle? A Leaderboard button opens the matching board: the day's difficulty for a daily, the overall board for a collection puzzle.
  • Next Puzzle takes you to your next unsolved puzzle, in collection order, or through today's Easy, Medium and Hard dailies. When everything's solved it says “All puzzles solved.”
