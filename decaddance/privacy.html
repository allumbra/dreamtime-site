Privacy Policy — Decaddance

Effective November 28, 2026 · Android and iOS apps com.decaddance.game

Decaddance is a game by Dreamtime LLC ("Dreamtime," "we," or "us"). This policy describes the information used by the Decaddance apps for Android and iOS, their cloud features, Google Play and App Store purchases and Google advertising services. It replaces the version effective October 2, 2026.

In short: you can play without an account. We collect only what the game's features need. Every ad is non-personalized, the game does not use your device's advertising ID, and we do not sell your personal information. You can ask us to see or delete your data at support@dreamti.me.

Local play and information on your device

Local gameplay, settings, saved matches and access to game features are stored on your device. Decaddance generates a random installation identifier for account synchronization; this is not your Android ID, the iOS identifier for vendor or an advertising identifier. Removing the app may remove local files, subject to your device's backup and restore settings. It does not itself delete cloud records.

Sign-in and your game account

Decaddance uses Google Play Games for authentication and Microsoft PlayFab for cloud game accounts. On Android, sign-in uses Google Play Games; on iPhone and iPad it uses Game Center. Without signing in, the game plays as a local guest, and the cloud features below apply only when a game account is signed in. When it starts, the game checks automatically for an existing Play Games sign-in (Android) or Game Center sign-in (iPhone and iPad). This can restore an existing Decaddance cloud account and start synchronization. Creating a new Decaddance cloud account requires an action by you, either tapping a sign-in button or entering an access code (see below); the automatic check does not create one. Local guest play remains available without a cloud account.

Game Center (iOS). On iPhone and iPad, signing in is optional. The game never shows Apple's Game Center sign-in screen unless you tap a sign-in button. If your device is already signed in to Game Center, the automatic check at launch uses that sign-in (Apple may show its "Welcome back" banner). The game then sends Apple's player identifier for this app (an identifier Apple scopes to our developer account) and Apple's sign-in proof to PlayFab, which checks it with Apple and uses it to find your game account. If you have none, nothing is created until you tap a sign-in button. The game does not read or send your Game Center name, friends or profile. On leaderboards, Game Center players always appear under an anonymous generated name.

The account services handle player identifiers and Google Play Games gamer profile information, such as a display name and provider-supplied avatar information. The game does not ask you for your Google password or payment-card details. Google Play Games also processes SDK analytics and diagnostics under Google's policies and account controls.

Access codes and guest accounts. Authorized app reviewers and testers can enter an access code we give them in Settings → Access code. If you are not signed in, the game then creates an anonymous guest cloud account for you. It uses a random identifier generated on your device and kept only in the app's private storage; no name, email address, password or device identifier is involved, and nothing is created unless you enter an access code. The guest account is created when you submit the code, before the code is checked, and the same identifier is reused for later tries. The code is sent securely to PlayFab, which checks it against stored one-way hashes of valid codes and, if it matches, records the Full Game unlock on that account. We keep how many times a code was used and, for your account, which code you redeemed and any failed attempts, so a code cannot be guessed or reused beyond its limit. If the code is wrong, that account is not used and holds only the times of the failed attempts; email support@dreamti.me to have it removed. A guest account is a game account like any other: the cloud features in this policy apply to it, you can later sign in with Google Play Games or Game Center, and Settings → Delete game account removes it. Because the identifier is the only key to a guest account, deleting the app's data without deleting the account leaves the account in the cloud with no way for you to sign in to it again. Decaddance does not ask for a username or password and does not offer public registration.

Cloud saves, daily results and leaderboards

For authenticated players, Decaddance sends its random installation identifier, account-associated product ownership, token balance history, daily completion records, results and progress to PlayFab. Cloud backups can include the game's commands and match snapshots, so play can be restored across sessions or devices. The app marks its uploaded replay backup records private.

Leaderboards can show your score, rank and display name or a stable player alias. This information is visible to other people viewing the leaderboard. A generated alias is a pseudonym, not a guarantee of anonymity. Private match backups are separate from these public leaderboard entries.

Leaderboards for signed-out players. A copy of the daily and lifetime leaderboards (rank, score and a generated alias, never your Google Play Games name) is published as public web files that anyone can read. GitHub hosts those files, so when the game fetches them GitHub receives your IP address, as any web host does (GitHub's privacy statement); the game sends no account or player identifier with the request. The copy is rebuilt on a schedule, not instantly. If you delete your game account, your alias and score can stay on that copy until its next successful rebuild after the change has taken effect. This is usually within a day but can take longer. Email support@dreamti.me to ask us to rebuild it sooner.

Purchases (Google Play and the App Store)

Google Play processes purchases on Android and Apple's App Store on iOS. The game receives product ownership and purchase-status information. On Android it supplies Google with an obfuscated account identifier, and on iOS it supplies Apple with a random app account token, to associate the transaction with the game account. Before a purchase unlocks anything, the game sends the store's purchase token (Google Play) or signed transaction (App Store) with the signed-in game account to PlayFab, which checks it with the store and records the verified product ownership. Decaddance stores product access locally and synchronizes these verified entitlements through PlayFab.

Decaddance does not receive your full payment-card number or bank details. Purchase records held by Google and Apple are subject to their policies. Deleting game data does not request a refund or delete the stores' transaction records.

Rewarded ads and ad privacy choices

Decaddance uses Google AdMob for rewarded ads that you choose to watch, for example to unlock another daily challenge, and Google's User Messaging Platform to check applicable consent and privacy choices. The consent information is refreshed when the game starts. Rewarded ad initialization and loading follow your request for an ad and the SDK's permission to request ads. Each finished rewarded ad adds a token to your game account. The game records when a token was earned and what it was spent on (a daily challenge, a practice game or a match level) so your balance is the same on each of your devices; for signed-in players this record is stored with your other game data in PlayFab and deleted with it. It contains no advertising identifiers.

Every ad is non-personalized, for every player in every region, whatever choice is recorded in the consent form. Non-personalized ads are chosen from context, such as the app and your general location, rather than from a profile of your interests or activity in other apps. The Android app does not request the advertising ID permission, so Google's SDK cannot read your device's advertising ID through it. The iOS app does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission, so the SDK does not get the iOS advertising identifier (IDFA).

Even for non-personalized ads, Google's advertising SDK can collect IP address information (including an estimate of general location), app interactions such as launches, taps and video views, performance diagnostics and SDK-related crash reports, and device-scoped identifiers that are not the advertising ID, such as Android's app set ID. Google uses this information to serve and measure ads, limit how often an ad is shown, and prevent fraud and abuse.

Where required and available, use Settings → Ad privacy choices to revisit the SDK-managed choices. Google separately provides ad preference controls.

Feedback you send

If you choose Settings → Send feedback, we receive the message you write, its kind (bug, idea, question or other) and, unless you switch it off, the app version, platform, device model, operating system, screen size, language and a few settings. We remove email addresses, links, phone numbers and addresses from the message on your device and again on our server, and we ask you not to include personal details. Reports are kept in a private issue tracker (GitHub, in the United States) until we remove them. A report carries no account identifier, only a one-way (salted hash) code that groups reports from the same account, which we cannot turn back into the account without our private key; PlayFab keeps a short record of your reports so we can limit how many you send and delete it with your account. Email support@dreamti.me to have a report removed.

Service providers and uses of information

Microsoft PlayFab hosts game-account, synchronization, purchase-verification and leaderboard services. Google provides Play Games authentication, Google Play billing, advertising and consent services. Apple provides Game Center sign-in and App Store purchases. GitHub stores the feedback you choose to send. Information is used to operate these features, associate progress and entitlements with the correct account, restore game state, display leaderboard results and support the providers' security, diagnostics and advertising functions.

The providers may process information outside your country. Their policies explain their own processing and controls:

  • Google Privacy Policy

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for targeted (cross-context behavioral) advertising. We share information only with the service providers named here, to run the game's features.

If you contact support, your message and the information you choose to provide are used to understand the request, identify the relevant game account and communicate with you. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes, session tickets, purchase tokens or full payment details.

Stored records and deletion requests

Cloud account records, saved gameplay, daily results, leaderboard statistics and entitlements are stored separately from your local game files. Uninstalling, clearing local data or signing out does not itself remove those cloud records.

Decaddance does not currently schedule automatic expiry of these cloud records solely because an account is inactive. They remain available for account recovery and game features until removed through account deletion or another supported data-removal process.

Settings → Account & privacy → Delete game account submits an authenticated request for the signed-in Decaddance account. The request queues removal of title-specific cloud saves, daily records, statistics, leaderboard entries and entitlement mirrors. The requesting device also clears that account's local game progress. Language, theme and audio preferences remain. A submitted request is not confirmation that the provider has finished processing it. The public copy of the leaderboards changes only at its next rebuild after that.

This title-specific operation does not remove PlayFab's publisher-level identity and linked sign-in accounts or its PlayStream event history. It also does not delete your Google account or Google's purchase records, and it does not issue a refund. Our external request route covers broader PlayFab data removal: we identify affected games and confirm the scope before removing data shared with other game accounts. Shared records needed for accounts you are keeping are handled separately. We do not promise a fixed automatic expiry for provider identity, event history, backups or support correspondence.

To request deletion of your Decaddance game account and associated data, follow the Decaddance deletion-request instructions or email support@dreamti.me. The request must identify Decaddance and the correct game account. Sending a request does not automatically erase data. You can send an external request even if you have uninstalled the game; you do not need to reinstall it to contact support. The request can also ask about provider-held records left outside the title-specific operation.

Google Play Games offers controls for its profile and game data through your Play Games profile and Google Account. Those controls do not by themselves delete a separate PlayFab game account.

Children

Decaddance is meant for players aged 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Every ad is non-personalized for every player. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has created a Decaddance game account or given us personal information, email support@dreamti.me and we will delete it.

Your privacy rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to tell you what information we hold about your Decaddance game account, to give you a copy, to correct it or to delete it. Email support@dreamti.me and identify the game as Decaddance. We may need to confirm that the account is yours before we act, and we will not treat you differently for making a request.

European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland. Under the GDPR and the UK GDPR you also have the right to restrict or object to our processing, and to receive your data in a portable format. We use your information to provide the game and the features you ask for (such as cloud saves, leaderboards and purchases), to keep the service secure and prevent fraud, and to show ads in line with the choices you make in the consent form, which you can change at any time in Settings. You can complain to your local data protection authority. We answer requests within one month.

California and other US states. Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (as amended by the CPRA) and similar state laws, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, use and disclose, and to request its deletion or correction. We do not sell or share personal information for targeted advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of. We do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you. An authorized agent can make a request for you. We answer requests within 45 days.

Security

The identified game cloud requests use HTTPS. Google's Mobile Ads and Play Games SDKs document encryption in transit for the data they collect. Account authentication protects access to account-associated game records. No method of transmission or storage provides an absolute security guarantee.

Changes to this policy

When the game starts collecting or using information in a new way, we update this policy first and change its effective date. Features that need personal data stay optional.
