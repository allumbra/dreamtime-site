Privacy Policy — Decaddance

Effective October 2, 2026 · Android app com.decaddance.game

Decaddance is a Dreamtime game ("Dreamtime," "we," or "us"). This policy describes information used by the Android game, its cloud features, Google Play purchases and Google advertising services.

Local play and information on your device

Local gameplay, settings, saved matches and access to game features are stored on your device. Decaddance generates a random installation identifier for account synchronization; this is not your Android ID. Removing the app may remove local files, subject to your device's backup and restore settings. It does not itself delete cloud records.

Google sign-in and your game account

Decaddance uses Google Play Games for authentication and Microsoft PlayFab for cloud game accounts. On Android, the game checks for an existing Play Games sign-in automatically. This can restore an existing Decaddance cloud account and start synchronization. Creating a new Decaddance cloud account requires an explicit sign-in action; the automatic check does not create one. Local guest play remains available without a cloud account.

The account services handle player identifiers and Google Play Games gamer profile information, such as a display name and provider-supplied avatar information. The game does not ask you for your Google password, a separate app password or payment-card details. Google Play Games also processes SDK analytics and diagnostics under Google's policies and account controls.

Cloud saves, daily results and leaderboards

For authenticated players, Decaddance sends its random installation identifier, account-associated product ownership, daily completion records, results and progress to PlayFab. Cloud backups can include the game's commands and match snapshots, so play can be restored across sessions or devices. The app marks its uploaded replay backup records private.

Leaderboards can show your score, rank and display name or a stable player alias. This information is visible to other people viewing the leaderboard. A generated alias is a pseudonym, not a guarantee of anonymity. Private match backups are separate from these public leaderboard entries.

Google Play purchases

Google Play processes purchases. The native Billing integration receives product ownership and purchase-status information and uses a purchase token to acknowledge a purchase with Google. It supplies Google with an obfuscated account identifier to associate the transaction with the game account. Decaddance stores product access locally and synchronizes account-associated entitlements through PlayFab.

Decaddance does not receive your full payment-card number or bank details. Purchase records held by Google are subject to Google's policies. Deleting game data does not request a refund or delete Google's transaction records.

Rewarded ads and ad privacy choices

Decaddance uses Google AdMob for user-requested rewarded ads and Google's User Messaging Platform to check applicable consent and privacy choices. The consent information is refreshed when the game starts. Rewarded ad initialization and loading follow an ad request and the SDK's permission to request ads. Rewards in this version are recorded by the game locally.

Google's advertising SDK can collect and share IP address information (including an estimate of general location), app interactions such as launches, taps and video views, performance diagnostics, and device or account-associated identifiers such as advertising ID and app-set ID. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Choosing non-personalized ads does not necessarily stop this processing.

Where required and available, use Settings → Ad privacy choices to revisit the SDK-managed choices. Android's advertising settings also provide advertising-ID controls. Google separately provides ad preference controls.

Service providers and uses of information

Microsoft PlayFab hosts game-account, synchronization and leaderboard services. Google provides Play Games authentication, Google Play billing, advertising and consent services. Information is used to operate these features, associate progress and entitlements with the correct account, restore game state, display leaderboard results and support the providers' security, diagnostics and advertising functions.

The providers may process information outside your country. Their policies explain their own processing and controls:

  • Microsoft Privacy Statement
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google advertising information

If you contact support, your message and the information you choose to provide are used to understand the request, identify the relevant game account and communicate with you. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes, session tickets, purchase tokens or full payment details.

Stored records and deletion requests

Cloud account records, saved gameplay, daily results, leaderboard statistics and entitlements are stored separately from your local game files. Uninstalling, clearing local data or signing out does not itself remove those cloud records.

Decaddance does not currently schedule automatic expiry of these cloud records solely because an account is inactive. They remain available for account recovery and game features until removed through account deletion or another supported data-removal process.

Settings → Account & privacy → Delete game account submits an authenticated request for the signed-in Decaddance account. The request queues removal of title-specific cloud saves, daily records, statistics, leaderboard entries and entitlement mirrors. The requesting device also clears that account's local game progress. Language, theme and audio preferences remain. A submitted request is not confirmation that the provider has finished processing it.

This title-specific operation does not remove PlayFab's publisher-level identity and linked sign-in accounts or its PlayStream event history. It also does not delete your Google account or Google's purchase records, and it does not issue a refund. Our external request route covers broader PlayFab data removal: we identify affected games and confirm the scope before removing data shared with other game accounts. Shared records needed for accounts you are keeping are handled separately. We do not promise a fixed automatic expiry for provider identity, event history, backups or support correspondence.

To request deletion of your Decaddance game account and associated data, follow the Decaddance deletion-request instructions or email support@dreamti.me. The request must identify Decaddance and the correct game account. Sending a request does not automatically erase data. You can send an external request even if you have uninstalled the game; you do not need to reinstall it to contact support. The request can also ask about provider-held records left outside the title-specific operation.

Google Play Games offers controls for its profile and game data through your Play Games profile and Google Account. Those controls do not by themselves delete a separate PlayFab game account.

Security

The identified game cloud requests use HTTPS. Google's Mobile Ads and Play Games SDKs document encryption in transit for the data they collect. Account authentication protects access to account-associated game records. No method of transmission or storage provides an absolute security guarantee.

Contact

For questions about Decaddance or its information handling, contact support@dreamti.me. Identify the game as Decaddance so the request is not confused with another Dreamtime game.
