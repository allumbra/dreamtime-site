Request deletion of your Decaddance account

Decaddance · Dreamtime · Android app com.decaddance.game

This page describes how to send a request to delete a Decaddance game account and its associated cloud data. It is not an automatic deletion form. You can send a request without reinstalling the game. Sending an email, uninstalling the app or signing out does not itself erase server records.

In-app game-data deletion

Use Settings → Account & privacy → Delete game account. It identifies the signed-in game account and requires an acknowledgement and separate confirmation. If the request cannot be completed in the app, use the email route below.

The title-scoped operation removes Decaddance cloud saves, daily results, leaderboard data and entitlement mirrors. Local game progress is also removed on the requesting device. Google accounts and Google Play purchases are not deleted or refunded. PlayFab retains its publisher identity, sign-in links and PlayStream service-event history. The email route below also covers requests concerning these records. Complimentary game access may be lost; language, theme and audio preferences remain on the requesting device.

Send a Decaddance-specific request

  1. Email support@dreamti.me with the subject Decaddance account deletion request.
  2. Say you are requesting deletion of your Decaddance PlayFab game account and associated cloud data. Include the Android package com.decaddance.game. A request for Decaddance should not be treated as a request to delete a Patida or another Dreamtime game account.
  3. If you already know your Decaddance game account ID, include it. The signed-in app shows it in Settings → Account & privacy, but you do not need to reinstall the app to initiate a request. This is the game account identifier, not your password, Google account ID or purchase token. If you do not know it, say so; do not guess an ID. A gamer display name and approximate play dates may help locate records but do not prove ownership.
  4. Say whether you used more than one Google Play Games profile with Decaddance, and which game account the request concerns. Mention any prior display name that may help locate the correct account.

Open an email request

If your device does not open an email app, copy the address and subject into your preferred mail service.

Protect your account information

Do not send passwords, authentication codes, PlayFab session tickets, Google authorization codes, purchase tokens or full card/bank details. A display name can change or resemble someone else's, and an email sender address alone does not prove ownership of a Google-linked game account. We verify account ownership before processing a request. We may ask for additional information or offer an authenticated verification step; initiating your request does not require installing the app.

Identify the data you want deleted

An account-and-associated-data request should cover the Decaddance cloud account, private gameplay backups, daily completion/results, game statistics and leaderboard entries, random installation identifiers stored with that account, and account-associated product entitlements. This describes the requested scope; it is not confirmation that processing has been completed.

If you are asking about only a particular game record instead of deleting the account, describe that clearly. Do not assume that removing one saved match also removes your account or other cloud data.

Timing and records outside title deletion

PlayFab processes title deletion asynchronously. A request acknowledgement is not a completion receipt; processing can take several minutes or longer. The app does not set an automatic inactivity expiry for cloud game records. Title deletion excludes publisher-level identity, sign-in links and PlayStream event history, and no fixed expiry is promised here for those records, provider backups or support correspondence. For broader removal, we check which game accounts share the provider identity. We explain the affected games and obtain confirmation before a deletion that would remove data from other games. If shared records must remain for accounts you are keeping, or provider assistance is needed, we explain the remaining scope and next steps.

Local files, purchases and provider accounts

Local game files and device backups are separate from the cloud account. Your device's uninstall, app-data and backup controls govern those copies. Google Play purchase/refund records and your Google account are not deleted by deleting Decaddance data. Use Google's own controls for Google-managed data and transactions.

The automatic sign-in check restores existing accounts only. Explicitly signing in again after a deletion can create new account records. Deleting a Google Play Games profile or game data through Google does not by itself delete the app's separate PlayFab account.
