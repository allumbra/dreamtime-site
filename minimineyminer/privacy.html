Privacy Policy — Miner Sweeper

Effective date: October 29, 2026

Miner Sweeper is a voxel 3D Minesweeper puzzle game for Android and iOS. It is developed and published by Dreamtime LLC, a Washington limited liability company that publishes games under the name Dreamtime Games ("Dreamtime," "we," or "us").

This policy explains what information is handled when you play Miner Sweeper, use its online features, make purchases, redeem a code, or watch an advertisement. The game is made to be safe for players of every age, including children, so it is built to use as little information as possible. Some features described here are available only on certain platforms. You can also read this policy inside the game, under Settings → Privacy policy.

In Short

  • You do not need an account, a name, or an email address to play. Everything works as a guest.
  • The game does not ask for your real name. Leaderboards show a generated nickname, never a real name, and you can switch your nickname off in Settings → Account so other players do not see it.
  • Signing in with Game Center (iOS) or Google Play Games (Android) is optional. It is never required, including to buy something.
  • Ads are optional rewarded ads. Every ad request is treated as child-directed, uses non-personalized ads, and does not use an advertising ID.
  • We do not use your information for tracking, profiling, or selling anything.
  • The game sends usage statistics, such as how many puzzles were played and how long a session lasted, and which link or Apple ad campaign led to the install (a campaign label, never a device or advertising identifier), linked only to your random game identifier, so we can improve the game and see which of our announcements work. They are never used for advertising and never sold. You can turn them off in Settings → Privacy → Share usage statistics.
  • You can send us feedback, such as a bug report or an idea, from inside the game. Please do not include your name, email, or address: the game removes anything that looks like contact details before sending it, and the feedback goes only to us, never to other players.
  • The game has no chat, no way to send anything to other players, and no access to your location, contacts, camera, or microphone. The daily puzzle's Share button only hands a short result text (no name, no nickname) to your device's own share menu, and you decide where it goes.
  • You can delete your data from inside the game at any time: Settings → Account → Delete my data.

Game Accounts and Sign-In

Miner Sweeper uses Microsoft PlayFab for online game services. The first time you play online, the game creates an anonymous guest account. The account is identified by a random game identifier that the app generates on your device. It is not made from any device identifier (such as the Android ID or Apple's identifier for vendor), it is not your advertising ID, and it contains no name, email address, or other personal details. Settings → Account shows your account's Player ID, a code you can include when you contact us about your account.

You can optionally link your game account to Game Center (iOS) or Google Play Games (Android) so that your game account, with your nickname, your leaderboard entries, the codes you redeemed, and, where your token balance is stored online, your tokens, can be recovered on another device or after you reinstall the game. Your game progress is not stored online (see below), so it is not part of this. If you do, we receive only the platform's player identifier and the verification data needed to link it to your game account. We do not receive your Apple or Google password, and we do not use your Game Center or Google Play Games name, picture, or friends in the game. Signing in is never required to play, to use leaderboards, or to make a purchase. Because a child's account on these services is managed by a parent or guardian, we recommend that a parent decide whether a child signs in.

Gameplay, Progress, and Leaderboards

Settings such as controls, graphics, and audio stay on your device. Your game progress (per-puzzle play and clear counts, best times, daily puzzle results and streak, and your deepest Infinite Well record) and the puzzles you have started but not finished are also kept only on your device. They are not sent to our servers, so they are not restored on a new device or after you reinstall the game. Earlier versions of the game also saved a copy of the progress with your game account; the current version deletes that copy the next time it goes online.

With online features on, the game sends the following to PlayFab, linked to your anonymous game account:

  • the results of the daily puzzle you submit: the date, difficulty, completion time, number of tries, and puzzle identifier;
  • your token balance and spending, tokens earned, and the items and puzzle packs you have unlocked; and
  • the record of rewarded ads you have watched, as described below.

The daily puzzle leaderboard shows each ranked player's generated nickname, difficulty, time, and rank to other players. Nicknames are made by the game from a fixed list of safe words and a number (for example, "Brave Miner 4821"). The game gives you one the first time you play online, and you can change it in Settings → Account only to another choice from the same lists; it is stored with your game account so the leaderboards can show it. The nickname is shown by default; you can turn off Show my nickname on leaderboards in Settings → Account at any time. The game then replaces the name stored with your game account by a generic code (for example "Hidden-48213977") that no leaderboard displays, and other players see an unrelated made-up name in its place. Turning the switch back on restores your nickname. If you change the switch while offline, it takes effect the next time the game is online. The game never asks for or shows a real name, and players cannot type free text, upload pictures, or message each other. Leaderboard entries are tied to your anonymous game account, which we can see but which does not identify you personally.

Usage Statistics

To understand how the game is played and make it better (for example, to find puzzles that are too hard, places where players get stuck, or parts of the game that do not work as intended), the game records usage statistics and sends them to our service provider, Microsoft PlayFab. We use them only for this analysis. They are never used for advertising or to build profiles, never shared with advertisers, and never sold. We do not use a separate analytics or crash-reporting company.

Usage statistics are linked to your random game identifier (the anonymous game account described above), not to a name, an email address, or a device identifier. They contain only:

  • when the game was opened and how long each play session lasted;
  • which puzzle was started and how it ended (for example cleared, an explosion, or left unfinished), with the mode, difficulty, number of attempts, time on the game clock, and how many blocks were dug and flags placed;
  • tokens spent and earned and why (for example a daily puzzle start or a reward), whether a rewarded ad was watched to the end, when the Store was opened, and the product and result of a purchase attempt (bought, cancelled, failed, pending, or restored), never any payment details;
  • how many How to Play pages were viewed and how many different screens were visited in a session;
  • where the game was installed from, once per installation: the short campaign label of the link that led to the install (for example "reddit-2611", a name we choose for each of our own links), or, for an Apple Ads ad, the numeric campaign, ad group and keyword identifiers of our campaign, whether it was a first download or a redownload, and how many hours passed between tapping the link or ad and installing. On Android this comes from Google Play's install referrer (the text of the link itself); the game keeps only our campaign labels and the two times and discards everything else, such as Google's ad click identifiers. On iOS the game asks Apple's AdServices service with a one-time token that Apple creates on your device for this purpose; Apple answers only whether one of our Apple Ads ads led to the install, and provides this without tracking you (no App Tracking Transparency prompt is involved). The game keeps only the identifiers listed above; the token is used once and not kept;
  • the campaign label of a redeem code you redeemed (see Redeem Codes); and
  • the game version, the platform (Android or iOS), and the date.

Usage statistics never contain your nickname, any text, your location, a device or advertising identifier, or an ad click identifier, and the information about where the game was installed from is used only to count how many players each of our links and campaigns brought and how much they play, never to show you ads or to follow you across other apps or websites.

Usage statistics are on by default. You can turn them off at any time in Settings → Privacy → Share usage statistics. When you turn them off, the game stops recording them at once and deletes the statistics it has not sent yet; turn them on again whenever you like. While you play offline, the statistics wait on your device (at most 30 days) and are sent the next time the game is online.

Feedback

You can send us feedback from inside the game, in Settings → Feedback → Send feedback or from the pause menu: you pick what it is about (a bug, an idea, a question, or something else) and write a short message. Sending feedback is always your choice. The game asks you not to include your name, email address, or home address, and before anything is sent it automatically replaces things that look like contact details (email addresses, phone numbers and other long numbers, web addresses and social media handles, street addresses, and phrases such as "my name is ...") with "[removed]". Our service removes them again before the feedback is stored.

If you leave Include game details on (it is on by default, and the form shows exactly what is included), the feedback also contains technical details that help us find and fix problems: the game version, the platform, the operating system version and the device model, the screen size and orientation, the language setting, which screen you were on, the game's settings, and, if you were in a puzzle, which puzzle it was with its difficulty, game clock and board, so we can recreate it. If usage statistics are on, it also contains your last few usage-statistics events (described above), and it contains the last technical error messages of the game, with folder names that could contain a name removed. It never contains your nickname, a device or advertising identifier, your location, or your contacts.

Feedback is sent through our game service (Microsoft PlayFab) to GitHub, which stores it for us as an issue in a private repository that only our team can see. It is not linked to your name (we never have one). It carries a scrambled reference made from your anonymous game identifier, not the identifier itself, so that we can group several reports from the same player, and so that we can answer you: an answer appears in the game under Settings → Feedback → Messages the next time the game goes online. We use feedback only to fix problems, improve the game, and answer you. It is never used for advertising, never shared with advertisers, and never sold. To prevent abuse, the game and our service limit how many reports can be sent in an hour and in a day, and our service keeps a short record of your recent reports (when they were sent and their reference numbers) with your anonymous game account.

While you are offline, feedback waits on your device and is sent the next time the game is online (it is discarded after 14 days if it cannot be sent).

Purchases

Google Play handles Android purchases, and Apple handles iOS purchases. Parents can control a child's purchases with Google Play's Family Link and Apple's Ask to Buy and Screen Time. The game and PlayFab receive only what is needed to deliver and restore what you bought: the product identifier, a purchase receipt or validation token, an order or transaction identifier, and the resulting token or unlock. On iOS, the purchase record Apple signs also states the purchase date, the store country and the price paid. We do not receive your name, payment-card details, or billing address from Google or Apple. Purchases can be restored through your Google Play or Apple account, whether or not you sign in to Game Center or Google Play Games. Pending purchase records are kept on your device until processing completes.

Redeem Codes

Settings → Account → Redeem a code lets you enter a code we hand out, for example to app reviewers or as a gift, to unlock puzzle packs or tokens. The code you type is sent to our service (Microsoft PlayFab) only to check it. We do not keep the codes themselves, only a scrambled (hashed) form of each one. If the code works, a record of which code was used, the date, and what it unlocked is stored with your anonymous game account, so the unlock stays when you reinstall the game or sign in on another device. To stop codes from being guessed, the times of failed attempts are also kept with your game account for one hour. Some codes belong to a campaign (for example a code we gave a community or a video creator); if usage statistics are on, the game reports that campaign's label with the redemption so we can see which campaigns players come from. Redeeming a code needs no sign-in, name, or email address, and these records are deleted with Delete my data.

Advertising and Your Choices

Miner Sweeper offers optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob: you choose to watch an ad in exchange for a token. An ad is never shown automatically and never during a puzzle: it plays only when you tap to watch one, and you can play without ever watching one. The game offers an ad on the daily puzzle's screens when you are out of tokens (for example after an explosion in a daily puzzle), always as one optional choice next to the others. Players who have bought the full game (Complete) see no ad offers.

Because children may play, every ad request is handled the same way for every player:

  • it is marked as child-directed (for COPPA) and as from a user under the age of consent (for GDPR and similar laws);
  • the ad content rating is capped at G;
  • ads are non-personalized: they are not based on your interests, your activity in other apps, or an advertising profile; and
  • the game does not use or share an advertising ID (the Android advertising ID or Apple's advertising identifier, IDFA), and does not ask for permission to track you (there is no App Tracking Transparency prompt on iOS, because nothing tracks you across other companies' apps or websites).

Google still has to receive some technical information to deliver and measure an ad and to prevent fraud. This can include your IP address (which can indicate an approximate location), device and app information such as model, operating system version, and app version, how the ad was shown, and diagnostic information. For requests marked child-directed, Google limits how this information may be used; see Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Because every request is marked as coming from a user under the age of consent, the game does not show Google's consent form, and ads do not wait for a consent decision. At start-up the game does ask Google's consent service (User Messaging Platform) for its status, with that same under-age marking. Because ads are non-personalized and child-directed, there is no ad-personalization choice to make in the game. The game also does not send your anonymous game account identifier to Google with ad requests.

The same rules apply on Android and iOS.

Rewards for Ads

When you watch a rewarded ad, the game credits one token, up to five a day, and watching all five unlocks twelve hours of unlimited play. To do this, the game keeps a record of the date, how many ads you have watched that day, and when your unlimited window ends, and PlayFab keeps the same record with your anonymous game account when your token balance is stored online. The game counts these rewards itself on your device and, where your token balance is stored online, in your PlayFab game account. Our servers do not receive any record of your ads from Google.

Information We Do Not Collect

Miner Sweeper does not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, postal address, photos, videos, voice or audio, contacts, calendar, files, messages, health data, or precise location. It does not request the location, contacts, camera, or microphone permissions. The game has no chat and no way to send content to other players. The daily puzzle's Share button opens your device's own share menu with a short result text (the date, difficulty, time, number of tries, and a link to the game; no name or nickname); the game does not see or record where you share it. The only text you can send is optional feedback to us, from which contact details are removed automatically (see Feedback). This does not prevent service providers from receiving network information such as an IP address, or from estimating a general location from it.

Microsoft (PlayFab and Azure), GitHub, Google, and Apple may process information in the United States and other countries, which can be outside your country. Where the law requires safeguards for such transfers, we rely on the data processing terms and safeguards these providers offer. Their handling of information is also described in Microsoft's Privacy Statement, GitHub's Privacy Statement, Google's Privacy Policy, and Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children’s Privacy

Miner Sweeper is a family-friendly game that children, including children under 13, may play. We treat every player as a child: there is no age screen and every player gets the same protections. The game does not use any information for behavioral advertising, tracking, or profiling, and it does not give children a way to chat, post, or share personal information with anyone.

The only information the game handles from a child is the minimum needed to run the game: an anonymous game identifier, daily puzzle results, a generated leaderboard nickname, token, purchase, and redeemed-code records, usage statistics (unless they are turned off; they include which of our links or ad campaigns led to the install, as a campaign label, never an advertising identifier), feedback the child chooses to send (with contact details removed automatically before it is stored), and a limited amount of technical information that Google needs to deliver and secure a rewarded ad (as described above). We use this information only to operate, maintain, and improve the game, such as running leaderboards, delivering purchases and rewards, preventing fraud, fixing errors, and understanding how the game is played. This is the kind of use the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) calls "support for the internal operations" of an app. We do not knowingly ask a child for, or collect from a child, personal information such as a name, email address, or location, and we do not sell it or use it for any other purpose.

Parents and guardians can delete their child's game account and its data from inside the game (Settings → Account → Delete my data), or contact us at support@dreamti.me to ask what information we hold about their child's game account, to have it deleted, or to refuse any further collection of it. See Data Retention and Deletion for both ways. We may ask for information to confirm that the account is your child's. If you believe a child has given us personal information by some other means, contact us and we will delete it.

Purchases and optional sign-in involve Google or Apple accounts, which a parent or guardian manages for a child. Parents can use Google Play Family Link and Apple Family Sharing, Ask to Buy, and Screen Time to approve or block purchases and to control sign-in.

Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your anonymous game account and its daily results, nickname, purchase, token, redeemed-code, and reward records until you delete them. We do not automatically delete inactive accounts: your data stays until you delete it in the game (Settings → Account → Delete my data) or ask us to delete it by email, as described below. Some records are short-lived by design: each daily leaderboard starts fresh every day (UTC), uploads that cannot be delivered are discarded from your device's waiting list after three days, failed redeem-code attempts are forgotten after one hour, feedback is kept for up to 12 months after we receive it and then deleted (the record of recent reports kept with your game account holds only the most recent ones), usage statistics are kept by our service provider for about 30 days and then deleted (we may keep overall totals, such as how many puzzles were cleared on a day, that are not linked to any game identifier), and the list of your recent daily clears kept on our service holds only the last 40 days. Google, Apple, and Microsoft also apply their own retention practices.

You, or a parent or guardian, can delete the game account and its data at any time, in two ways:

  • In the game: open Settings → Account → Delete my data and confirm twice. This needs an internet connection. It deletes your game account on our service and everything stored with it: your daily puzzle results and leaderboard entries, your nickname, the codes you redeemed and what they unlocked, and the tokens and purchase and reward records on that account. If you signed in with Game Center or Google Play Games, the game first removes that link from your game account. It also deletes the usage statistics and any feedback on your device that have not been sent yet, the record of your feedback reports kept with your game account, and the developer's answers to your feedback. You can also choose to erase the progress kept on the device. The game then starts again with a new guest account and a new nickname. Things you bought through Google Play or the App Store are not lost: use Restore purchases in the Store to get them back from Google or Apple. Tokens cannot be restored.
  • By email: write to support@dreamti.me. Settings → Account shows your Player ID; please include it. If you cannot open the game, tell us the platform (Android or iOS), your leaderboard nickname if you know it, the approximate dates you played, and, if you signed in, whether you used Game Center or Google Play Games. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, or payment-card details. We may ask for information to verify that the account belongs to you or your child, and we will explain any records that must be retained and why. We aim to answer within 30 days.

Deleting removes the game account and its data, and our service provider usually completes it within minutes. Feedback that was already sent is not removed from our private feedback repository by the in-game deletion (it contains no contact details and no game identifier, only the scrambled reference described above, and is deleted after 12 months at the latest); email us with your Player ID and we will remove it sooner. Usage statistics that were already sent are not removed by the in-game deletion; they stay linked only to the deleted account's random identifier and are deleted automatically after about 30 days. If you ask us by email, we can delete them sooner. The random identifier the app used to sign in may remain on the provider's account record without any game data attached, and the app never uses it again. Microsoft PlayFab may keep limited service logs, such as a history of service events like sign-ins, under Microsoft's own terms and retention practices, which we do not control. Google and Apple keep their own records of purchases.

Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device (subject to your device's backup settings) but does not itself delete the account records on our servers: use Delete my data in the game first, or email us. Requests concerning your Google, Apple, or Microsoft account, or your Game Center or Google Play Games data, should also be sent to that provider.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to correct or delete it, to object to or restrict how it is used, and to receive a copy of it. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. You can delete your data yourself in the game (Settings → Account → Delete my data). To use any of these rights, email support@dreamti.me. A parent or guardian can do this on behalf of a child. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you can also complain to your local data protection authority. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time, and doing so does not affect what happened before.

Security

Miner Sweeper uses encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections for its online services. Players cannot write directly to leaderboards, purchases, or token balances; those are checked and applied by our service. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.
